## What we compound

- **Carbon-fiber-reinforced grades** at 10–40% loading, using chopped fiber from our own fiber lines where it suits the application.
- **Glass-fiber and mineral-filled grades** for stiffness and dimensional stability.
- **Tribological grades** with PTFE, MoS₂ or graphite for bearings, bushings, seals and wear parts.
- **ESD grades** for static-dissipative parts.

Compounds can be built on recovered resin, virgin resin or a defined blend of both, and are validated against mechanical, thermal and rheological targets agreed with you.

## How development works

1. **Specification.** You tell us the base polymer, the target properties or a reference grade, the application and the volume.
2. **Formulation at R&D scale** in South Jordan, Utah, with small-scale reactive extrusion.
3. **Samples and data** for your qualification.
4. **Scale-up** at our Sumter, South Carolina plant, in Croatia or at the Kandla extrusion site in India.
5. **Production lots** under an agreed specification, each with its Certificate of Analysis.

## Toll compounding

We take on toll compounding case by case when capacity allows. Tell us the material, the volume and the specification.
