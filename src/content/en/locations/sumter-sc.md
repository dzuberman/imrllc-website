## What happens in Sumter

Sumter is IRM's large-scale production facility in the United States.

- **Collection** of ballistic and technical fiber waste in volume, and **deconstruction** of end-of-life body armor and ballistic panels.
- **Cleaning** — solvent and other cleaning processes that recover high-performance aromatic polymers and fibers, from PEEK to para-aramid.
- **Micronization** of PTFE and post-cured PAI into fine powder.
- **Thermoset recovery**, including powder from thermoset polyimide such as Vespel®.
- **Industrial-scale reactive extrusion** of PEEK, PPS, PA, LCP and fluoropolymer compounds, using fibers, powders and additives from our South Jordan, Utah technical hub.

Together with South Jordan, the plant forms one US chain from collected waste to tested compound pellet.
