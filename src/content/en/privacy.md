## Who we are

This website is operated by **International Resources Manufacturing LLC** ("IRM", "we", "us"), 881 W. Baxter Dr., Suite 100, South Jordan, UT 84095, USA. IRM is part of the group of companies led by Global Consulting & Manufacturing Group S.L. (GCMG), C/ Sant Salvador 10A, Pis 0 4-8, AD500 Andorra la Vella, Andorra.

For any privacy question or request, email **[info@irmllc.co](mailto:info@irmllc.co)**.

## What we collect

**Information you send us.** When you use the inquiry form or email us, we receive what you provide: your name, company, email address, phone number, country, the material and quantity you describe, your message, and any photos or documents you attach.

**Technical information.** Our hosting and security provider processes standard technical data — IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites. The site's fonts and scripts are served from our own domain.

## How we use it

- To answer your inquiry, prepare quotes and carry out the business that follows from it.
- To manage our business relationship with you and your company.
- To keep the website secure and working.
- To meet legal obligations, such as record-keeping and export and waste-shipment documentation.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

## Legal bases (EU, EEA, UK and similar laws)

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process your information because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform a contract, for our legitimate interest in running and securing our business and responding to business inquiries, or to comply with legal obligations.

## Who we share it with

- **Group companies.** Your inquiry may be handled by colleagues in other GCMG group companies — for example in Europe or Asia — when that is the best way to answer it.
- **Service providers** that process data on our behalf: our website hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) and our customer relationship management system (Zoho CRM).
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it.

Some of these recipients are outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records for as long as we need them to answer you and to manage any business relationship that follows, and for any longer period the law requires. We then delete or anonymize them.

## Security

Information sent through the form travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to inquiry records is limited to the people who need it to do their work.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to receive a copy in a portable format, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To make a request, email [info@irmllc.co](mailto:info@irmllc.co). If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

This website is intended for businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

We will post any changes to this notice on this page and update the date at the top.
