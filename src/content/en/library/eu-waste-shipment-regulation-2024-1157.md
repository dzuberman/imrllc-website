*This is a general summary for orientation, not legal advice. Check the regulation and your national authority's guidance for any specific shipment.*

## What changed

Regulation (EU) 2024/1157 replaces the EU's previous waste shipment regulation. It entered into force on 20 May 2024 and applies from **21 May 2026**. According to the European Commission, its main effects on plastic waste are:

| Shipment | Rule | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Within the EU — non-hazardous plastic waste for recovery (EU3011) | Exempt from the new controls; general information requirements apply | — |
| Within the EU — non-hazardous, hard-to-recycle plastic waste (EU48) | Prior written notification and consent | 21 May 2026 |
| Exports to OECD countries — including clean, non-hazardous plastic waste (B3011) | Prior written notification and consent; Commission monitoring of plastic waste exports | 21 May 2026 |
| Exports to non-OECD countries | Prohibited | 21 November 2026, at least until 21 May 2029 |

After the ban period, a non-OECD country can ask to be listed as a destination, but only for non-hazardous plastic waste (B3011) and only if it shows it can manage that waste in an environmentally sound way. Source: [European Commission — plastic waste shipments](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/waste-shipments/plastic-waste-shipments_en).

## Key dates

- **20 May 2024** — regulation in force.
- **21 May 2026** — regulation applies; prior notification and consent for plastic waste exports.
- **21 November 2026** — ban on plastic waste exports to non-OECD countries begins.
- **21 May 2027** — independent audit requirement applies to facilities in recipient countries.
- **21 May 2029** — earliest end of the non-OECD ban.

## What it means for sellers of high-performance scrap

- **Classify every lot correctly.** The waste code decides which procedure applies. Clean, single-polymer post-industrial scrap is treated differently from mixed or contaminated material.
- **Plan export lead times.** Notification and consent take time; recurring streams benefit from planning ahead.
- **Recovery inside the EU** stays the simplest route for EU generators of non-hazardous plastic scrap.
- **Keep records.** Chain-of-custody documentation is increasingly what customers and authorities ask for.
