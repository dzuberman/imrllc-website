## What post-cure does

Polyamide-imide (PAI) combines amide and imide linkages in its backbone. Torlon® resins are injection-molded or extruded as thermoplastics, then **post-cured**: parts spend an extended period in a staged heat cycle that builds molecular weight and develops their full strength, wear resistance and chemical resistance. The Torlon PAI design guide describes cure studies at 260 °C and notes that the right cycle length depends on part configuration, thickness and molding conditions. Stock-shape producers cure rod and plate before they ship, so machine-shop PAI scrap is normally cured.

After a full post-cure, PAI no longer melt-processes like a thermoplastic.

## Two scrap streams

| | Uncured PAI | Post-cured PAI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where it comes from | The molding press — runners, sprues, off-spec parts before the cure oven | The machine shop and after the oven — swarf, chips, rod and plate ends, cured parts |
| Can it be melted again? | Yes | No |
| Recovery route | Melt reprocessing | Micronization to fine powder |
| End use | Molding resin and compounds | Functional filler in compounds |

## Why mixing hurts

Cured particles in an uncured lot don't melt; they behave like contamination in the melt route. Uncured material in a cured lot is just a lower-value powder. A mixed lot ends up on the cheaper route, or needs sorting first.

## How to keep them apart

1. **Collect at the source.** Press-side scrap goes in one container; machine-shop scrap in another.
2. **Label the state** as well as the grade — "4203L runners, uncured" or "4301 rod ends, cured".
3. **Keep grades apart.** Torlon grades differ in fillers: 4203L is unfilled, 4301 contains 12% graphite and 3% PTFE, 5030 is 30% glass fiber and 7130 is 30% carbon fiber (Drake Plastics datasheets — see the [materials data reference](/materials/#imidized)).
4. **When unsure, tell us where it was collected.** That usually settles whether it is cured.

## How we recover it

Uncured PAI goes through the melt route. Post-cured PAI is micronized — the group does this in the US, Croatia and India — and the powder is used as a filler that adds wear and temperature resistance. See [PAI and Torlon scrap recycling](/pai-torlon-recycling/).
