## Why it matters

The three sulfone polymers share an aryl–SO₂–aryl backbone, natural amber transparency and amorphous structure. They are used in similar places — medical and dental instruments, fluid handling, food service, filtration — and often by the same molders. But they differ in temperature resistance, toughness and price, and they don't behave like one material when mixed. A lot sold as one sulfone that turns out to be another is worth something different and may need a different route.

## The numbers that separate them

| Polymer | Common trade names | Reference grade | Tg (°C) | Density (g/cm³) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PSU | Udel®, Ultrason® S | Udel P-1700 | 185 | 1.24 |
| PESU | Veradel®, Ultrason® E | Veradel 3200P | 220 | 1.37 |
| PPSU | Radel®, Ultrason® P | Radel R-5000 | 220 | 1.29 |

Sources: Syensqo Udel PSU design guide and Udel P-1700 datasheet; Veradel 3200P data; Radel R-5000 datasheet — see the [materials data reference](/materials/#sulfones).

Reading the table:

- **DSC glass transition** separates PSU (185 °C) from the other two (220 °C). Because the sulfones are amorphous, DSC shows a glass transition and no melting peak.
- **Density** separates PESU (1.37) from PPSU (1.29) — the two share a glass transition, so Tg alone cannot tell them apart. A simple density measurement on unfilled material does.
- **Spectroscopy** confirms identity when fillers, pigments or blends make density unreliable.

## Look-alikes outside the family

**PEI** (ULTEM®) is also amber, transparent and amorphous. Its glass transition — 217 °C for ULTEM 1000 — sits close to PESU and PPSU, so a single DSC run can mislead; density (1.27 for ULTEM 1000) and spectroscopy help. Keep PEI scrap clearly separated from sulfones at the source.

## Segregation at the source

1. Give each sulfone its own container, labeled with the grade name.
2. Keep glass-filled grades separate from unfilled ones.
3. Keep membrane and filter waste separate from molding scrap — it may contain other materials.
4. If you don't know what a stream is, say so: we test it before quoting a final price.
