## What the service covers

Producing high-performance polymers creates by-products: powders and fines, lumps from start-ups and grade changes, transition material and off-spec lots. They are often too valuable to landfill but don't fit a producer's own sales channels. We take them off your hands and put them to use — in our own recovery and compounding operations, or with qualified processors in our network.

Examples of what we collect today include **ECTFE powder** and **PPA and LCP lumps**, alongside other fluoropolymer, PAEK, sulfone and polyamide by-products. We work with large polymer and petrochemical producers.

## How it works

1. **Confidential assessment.** Under NDA if required, you tell us the material, form, volume and frequency.
2. **Sampling and testing** to agree a specification and pricing basis.
3. **Scheduled collection** with packaging and logistics agreed for your site.
4. **Documented handling** — chain of custody and, where you require it, agreed rules on where material may and may not be sold.

## Why producers work with us

- One partner for many polymers and forms, rather than one buyer per material.
- In-house cleaning, micronization, extrusion and compounding across the US, Europe and Asia.
- Discretion: material routing can be restricted by product, market or region on request.

Off-take is our priority; toll processing for producers is possible case by case.
