## Methods we use

| Method | What it measures | Used for | Standard (confirm) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DSC | Glass transition, melting point, crystallinity, cold crystallization | Polymer identification; crystallinity of PAEK lots | [ISO 11357 / ASTM D3418] |
| TGA | Filler, fiber and ash content; decomposition profile | Filled-grade identification; contamination | [ISO 11358] |
| Melt flow (MFR/MVR) | Flow at a defined temperature and load | Molecular-weight change between cycles; blend ratios | [ISO 1133 / ASTM D1238] |
| Density | Specific gravity | Separating look-alikes such as PESU and PPSU | [ISO 1183 / ASTM D792] |
| Ash | Inorganic residue after combustion | Filler content checks | [method] |
| Fiber characterization | Length, fineness, sizing content | Chopped carbon fiber and recycled aramid | [methods] |
| Spectroscopy | Chemical identity | Confirming look-alike polymers | [FTIR / NIR — confirm] |
