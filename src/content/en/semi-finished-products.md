## Why semi-finished products

IRM began with recovering and compounding high-performance polymers and fibers. Adding semi-finished products means one group can now collect scrap, recover it, compound it and turn it into stock shapes and textiles — with testing and traceability at every step. The division is relatively new and its range is growing.

## Product range

### Polymer rods

Rods in **PEEK, PESU, PPS** and other high-performance polymers, for machining shops and OEMs. Tell us the polymer, grade, diameter, length and quantity you need, and whether recycled-content material is acceptable for your application.

### Nonwoven fire blankets

Nonwoven fire blankets made from high-performance fibers such as para-aramid, PBO, PBI, oxidized PAN and phenolic fibers and their blends — materials that resist heat and do not melt or drip.

### Recycled yarns

Yarns spun from recovered high-performance fibers, alone or in blends — a way to put para-aramid and other recovered fibers back into textiles.
