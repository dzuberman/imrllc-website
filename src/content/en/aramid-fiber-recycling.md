## Ballistic and coated para-aramid

End-of-life body armor and ballistic panels contain large amounts of para-aramid, often coated or laminated with rubber, phenolic or PVB resins. Our Sumter, South Carolina plant collects ballistic and technical fiber waste in volume and deconstructs end-of-life armor and panels. Coated and resin-bound offcuts are cleaned by solvent processes in the US, Croatia and India to free the fiber for reuse.

## What recovered fiber becomes

After cleaning and opening, para-aramid takes one of several routes:

- **Pulp** — for friction materials, gaskets and compound reinforcement.
- **Precision-cut fiber** — for reinforcement in compounds.
- **Staple and recycled yarn** — spun alone or in blends with other high-performance fibers.
- **Nonwovens** — including nonwoven fire blankets from our [semi-finished products division](/semi-finished-products/).

UHMWPE from ballistic PPE is also processed into pulp. Because drawn UHMWPE and para-aramid are extremely tough, they are pulped, precision-cut or, where powder is needed, ground cryogenically.

## Other high-performance fibers

| Fiber | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| PBO (Zylon®) | Very high strength and modulus; sensitive to UV light and moisture, so it is stored dry and dark |
| PBI fiber | Does not melt; limiting oxygen index above 41% ([PBI fiber datasheet](https://www.minifibers.com/files/TD/TDS_PBI-Fibers.pdf)) |
| Oxidized PAN (preox, OPAN) | Stabilized PAN fiber used directly for flame and heat protection; brands include PANOX®, ZOLTEK™ OX and PYROMEX® |
| Phenolic (novoloid, Kynol™) | Does not melt; low smoke and combustion toxicity; specific gravity 1.27 ([datasheet](https://transportationblog.polyone.com/sites/default/files/2021-06/fl.a4.datasheet-novoloid.pdf)) |
| Meta-aramid (Nomex®, Conex®) | Heat-protective fiber for PPE and filtration — a different molecule from para-aramid |

These fibers are cleaned, opened and blended into yarns and nonwovens, alone or with para-aramid.
