These materials are **not used in our own compounding**. They make up a trading book that complements our core activities, handled with the same testing, classification and cross-border documentation as our high-performance streams. We work directly with generators — machine shops, molders and OEMs — rather than through broker chains.

| Material | Typical sources | Example trade names |
| --- | --- | --- |
| POM-C, POM-H (acetal) | Gear, automotive and consumer-product machining and molding | Hostaform®, Delrin® |
| PC (polycarbonate) | Injection scrap, sheet trim, lighting and housings | Lexan®, Makrolon® |
| PC/ABS | Automotive interiors, electronics and IT housings | Cycoloy®, Bayblend® |
| PC/ASA | Exterior trim and outdoor housings | Geloy® |

Trade names identify typical materials only; IRM is not affiliated with their owners.

## How trading works

Send photos, the material and grade if known, the form, the quantity and the location. We test where needed, agree a price and logistics, classify the material for its destination, and ship with chain-of-custody documentation. For high-performance polymers, see [what we buy](/sell-scrap/).
