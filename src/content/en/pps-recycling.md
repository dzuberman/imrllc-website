## What we recycle

PPS is a semi-crystalline polymer used for chemical-resistant and dimensionally stable parts — pump and valve components, electrical connectors, automotive parts and machined stock. A Ryton® PPS datasheet lists a glass transition of 90 °C, a melting point of 280 °C and a specific gravity of 1.34 for the unfilled polymer ([materials data reference](/materials/#others)). We buy:

- **Machining stock ends, tube and ring offcuts, and swarf** from PPS rod, plate and tube.
- **Molding scrap** — runners, sprues and rejected parts.
- **Filled compounds** — glass-fiber, carbon-fiber and lubricated grades, kept separate by grade.

Large lots of machined-shape leftovers are welcome; send photos and the grade name, and tell us where the material is located.

## How PPS scrap is recovered

PPS follows the **melt route**: intake testing (DSC for melting point and crystallinity, TGA for filler content), cleaning where needed, drying, size reduction and twin-screw extrusion to repelletized granule or compound. Filled grades are reprocessed as compounds; their filler content is measured and reported on the Certificate of Analysis.

## PPS in our product range

PPS compounds (including carbon- and glass-fiber grades) are part of our [compounding](/compounding/) range, and PPS rods are produced by our [semi-finished products division](/semi-finished-products/).
