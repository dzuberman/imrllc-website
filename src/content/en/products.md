## Forms we supply

| Form | What it is | Typical polymers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regrind | Mechanically size-reduced, cleaned scrap, sized to feed an extruder | PEEK, PEKK, PEI, sulfones, PPS, fluoropolymers |
| Repelletized granule | Regrind melted, homogenized, extruded and strand-cut into uniform pellets | PEEK, PEKK, PEI, sulfones, PPS, PVDF and other melt-processable fluoropolymers |
| Compound granule | Repelletized with fillers or reinforcement compounded in-line | CF-, GF-, tribological and ESD grades — see [compounding](/compounding/) |
| Micronized powder | Fine powder from materials that cannot melt | PTFE, post-cured PAI, thermoset polyimide, PBI |
| Chopped carbon fiber | Oversized precision-chopped (0.1–12 mm) or random-chopped fiber | PAN-based carbon fiber — see [carbon fiber](/carbon-fiber/) |
| Recycled para-aramid | Pulp, cut fiber, staple and yarn | Para-aramid and blends — see [aramid fibers](/aramid-fiber-recycling/) |
| Semi-finished products | Rods, nonwoven fire blankets, recycled yarns | See [semi-finished products](/semi-finished-products/) |

## Test data with every lot

Every shipment carries a **Certificate of Analysis** with DSC results (glass transition, melting point, crystallinity), melt flow, density, ash or filler content, and traceability to the source stream and processing run. Higher-tier materials can be supplied with a full **Material Analysis Record**. Safety Data Sheets and customs documents are included.

## Qualifying recycled material

Recycled high-performance polymers are used successfully in many industrial applications, but every application is different. We supply development samples with their test data so you can qualify the material in your own process, then schedule production lots against an agreed specification. Regulated uses — medical implants, flight-critical parts, food contact — require material certified for that use; tell us your requirements up front.

## How to order

Tell us the polymer, the form, any filler or reinforcement, the target properties or reference grade, the application and your expected volume. Availability of recycled grades depends on incoming scrap, so we confirm lead time and lot size with each quote.
