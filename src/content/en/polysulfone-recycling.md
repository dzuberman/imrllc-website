## What we recycle

The sulfone polymers — PSU, PESU and PPSU — are amorphous, hydrolytically stable and widely used where parts see hot water, steam sterilization or aggressive cleaning: medical and dental instruments, plumbing and fluid-handling components, food-service parts, aircraft interiors and filtration membranes. We buy:

- **Molding scrap** — runners, sprues, purge and rejected parts.
- **Extrusion scrap** — pipe, profile and sheet offcuts.
- **Membrane and filter production waste**, where the sulfone can be separated from other materials.
- **Regrind and off-spec pellets.**

## Telling PSU, PESU and PPSU apart

All three are transparent amber in their natural color, so they are easy to confuse in a scrap bin. Glass transition temperature and density separate them:

| Polymer | Reference grade | Tg (°C) | Density (g/cm³) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PSU | Udel P-1700 | 185 | 1.24 |
| PESU | Veradel 3200P | 220 | 1.37 |
| PPSU | Radel R-5000 | 220 | 1.29 |

PESU and PPSU share a 220 °C glass transition, so density — or spectroscopy — is what separates them. Sources are in the [materials data reference](/materials/#sulfones). We test every incoming lot; a stream sold as one sulfone sometimes turns out to be another, which changes both its value and its route.

Our guide [Identifying PSU, PESU and PPSU scrap](/library/identifying-psu-pesu-ppsu-scrap/) walks through the checks.

## How sulfone scrap is recovered

Sulfones follow the **melt route**: intake testing, cleaning where needed, thorough drying, size reduction and twin-screw extrusion to repelletized granule. Each sulfone is processed on its own — blends of PSU, PESU and PPSU do not behave like any one of them.

Polymer producers are also developing chemical recycling for this family: in 2025 Syensqo announced a process that breaks PSU, PPSU and PESU waste back down to monomers ([Renewable Carbon News](https://renewable-carbon.eu/news/syensqo-pioneers-innovative-recycling-technology-for-circular-sulfone-polymers/)). Mechanical recycling remains the direct route for clean, segregated post-industrial scrap.
