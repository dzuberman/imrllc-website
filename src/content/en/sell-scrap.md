prepare:
  eyebrow: Getting the best price
  title: How to prepare your scrap
  body: |
    Clean, single-grade, well-labeled scrap is worth more and moves faster.

    - **Segregate by polymer and grade.** Keep PEEK away from PEKK, PSU away from PESU and PPSU, uncured PAI away from post-cured PAI.
    - **Keep filled and unfilled grades apart.** Carbon-fiber, glass-fiber and lubricated grades each go in their own container.
    - **Keep it dry and covered,** away from metal, paper, floor sweepings and commodity plastics.
    - **Label every container** with the original grade name if you know it — for example "VICTREX 450G swarf" or "Torlon 4301 rod ends".
    - **Tell us about contact media.** Coolant- or oil-wet swarf can often be cleaned, but we need to know what it has touched.
