## What we do

IRM recovers, compounds and supplies high-performance polymers and technical fibers that most recyclers cannot process — PEEK and the other PAEKs, imidized polymers, sulfones, PPS, LCP, fluoropolymers, carbon fiber and para-aramid among them. We buy scrap and production by-products, recover them by the route each material needs, and deliver tested materials back to manufacturers and converters.

## How the group fits together

Together with **Global Consulting & Manufacturing Group S.L. (GCMG)** — the group holding and international operating arm — IRM is part of a vertically integrated platform covering collection, qualification, cleaning, fiber processing, compounding, trading and, most recently, semi-finished products.

- **United States (IRM LLC):** headquarters and technical fiber hub in South Jordan, Utah; industrial plant in Sumter, South Carolina; office in North Charleston, South Carolina.
- **Europe:** a fiber plant and a compound plant in Croatia.
- **Asia:** collection, cleaning and micronization in Rajkot, India; the main India extrusion site in the Kandla Special Economic Zone; a remelting and cleaning joint venture in Malaysia; purchasing and sales in Foshan, China, Tokyo, Japan and South Korea.
- **South America:** a technical waste collection hub in Brazil.

See the [global network](/global-network/) for every site.

## What sets us apart

**Fiber and compound, paired.** Fiber processing — chopping, pulping, powder production — sits alongside polymer compounding, so fiber length, binder and matrix formulation are controlled in one supply chain.

**Direct source relationships.** We work directly with machining houses, molders, stock-shape extruders, OEM suppliers and polymer producers rather than through broker chains.

**Regulated cross-border handling.** Waste classification, shipment notifications, customs documentation and chain-of-custody records for EU, North American and Asian destinations, with documentation to support customers' aerospace, defense and sustainability reporting.

**Vertical integration.** With our newer semi-finished products division, one group now covers the path from scrap to stock shapes and textiles.
